INDONESIAN ORIGINS

Discover Indonesia's Coffee Regions

From the highlands of Aceh to the volcanic soils of Flores — each origin offers distinct terroir and flavor profiles.

Aceh Gayo

Gayo Highlands

High-altitude Arabica from the Gayo Highlands. Known for clean, bright acidity with notes of citrus, jasmine, and brown sugar.

  • Varieties: Ateng, Tim Tim, Bor Bor
  • Process: Washed, Honey, Natural
  • Altitude: 1,200–1,600 MASL
  • Harvest: Oct–Mar / Jun–Sep
View Coffees →
Sumatra

Mandheling & Lintong

Earthy, full-bodied coffees with low acidity. Wet-hulled process creates signature herbal, spice, and dark chocolate notes.

  • Varieties: Typica, Catimor, Ateng
  • Process: Wet-Hulled (Giling Basah)
  • Altitude: 1,100–1,500 MASL
  • Harvest: Oct–Mar
View Coffees →
Java

Java Preanger & Ijen

Classic Indonesian profile — heavy body, low acidity, with notes of molasses, tobacco, and cedar. Estate-grown on historic plantations.

  • Varieties: Typica, USDA 762, S-795
  • Process: Washed, Natural
  • Altitude: 900–1,400 MASL
  • Harvest: Jun–Sep
View Coffees →
Sulawesi

Toraja & Kalosi

Complex, multi-dimensional cups with bright acidity, ripe fruit notes, and a distinctive clean finish. Smallholder farms.

  • Varieties: S-795, Typica, Catimor
  • Process: Washed, Wet-Hulled
  • Altitude: 1,200–1,800 MASL
  • Harvest: May–Sep
View Coffees →
Bali

Kintamani Highlands

Bright, citrusy Arabica grown in volcanic soil near Mt. Batur. Subak irrigation system creates unique terroir expression.

  • Varieties: S-795, Kartika, USDA 762
  • Process: Washed, Honey
  • Altitude: 900–1,300 MASL
  • Harvest: May–Oct
View Coffees →
Flores

Bajawa & Manggarai

Emerging specialty region with floral aromatics, stone fruit sweetness, and lingering finish. Small-scale organic farming.

  • Varieties: S-795, Catimor, Typica
  • Process: Washed, Natural
  • Altitude: 1,000–1,600 MASL
  • Harvest: May–Sep
